Skills

A robot is never one discipline. The soil rover needed a URDF, a Nav2 config, a PyQt dashboard, an NPK sensor driver and a plotting pipeline before it could do anything interesting — and that is a small project.

So this list is wide on purpose. Simulation and navigation are where I am deepest. Perception is where I am most curious. Embedded is what I learned first and keeps paying off. Infrastructure is what stops a good demo from staying a demo.

Deepest

ROS 2, Gazebo and Nav2 — the stack I reach for first, and the one I have debugged at 2am.

Most fun: vision transformers on cheap hardware.
