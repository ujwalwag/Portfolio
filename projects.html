Projects

Autonomy stacks, perception models, and hardware that had to survive a field.

These are not seven unrelated demos. Four of them are the same problem approached from different angles: how does a machine sense what soil and crops need, and then act on it without a person steering? The rest are the supporting skills — a vision model that has to be right, a gesture interface that makes teleop feel less like flying a drone, a tidying robot that has to plan around furniture.

Autonomy

Driftbot — Autonomous Tidying Robot

A four-wheeled differential-drive robot with a three-joint magnetic arm that finds objects scattered across a two-room home and returns them to a collection bin.

  • ROS 2 Jazzy
  • Gazebo Harmonic
  • ros_gz_bridge
  • URDF / Xacro
  • Bug2
  • Python
How it works
  • Mission 1 drives straight to a toy block, lifts it, turns around and drops it in the bin.
  • Mission 2 uses Bug2 logic to weave around furniture, reaching two further blocks one at a time.
  • Mission 3, in progress, pairs random exploration with computer vision to decide what to tidy.
  • Runs are followed live in RViz beside a camera feed.

Agriculture

Soil Fertility Detection Rover

A differential-drive farm rover with an arm and sensor suite, simulated in Gazebo Harmonic with Nav2-oriented navigation and driven from a PyQt supervisory GUI.

  • ROS 2
  • Gazebo Harmonic
  • Nav2
  • PyQt
  • ros2_control
How it works
  • Split into ROS 2 packages: robot description, worlds and bridges, navigation configs, arm bringup, GUI and top-level launches.
  • Launch profiles for simulation only, simulation with GUI, and full simulation with Nav2.
  • The GUI runs either autonomous missions or manual teleop.
  • Hardware paths are stubbed and waiting for bringup.

Perception

Hand Gesture Robot Control

Drive a TurtleBot3 around a Gazebo world with hand gestures from a webcam, recognised in real time at 98% accuracy by a SigLIP vision transformer.

  • ROS
  • Gazebo
  • SigLIP
  • OpenCV
  • Flask
  • TurtleBot3
How it works
  • Nineteen distinct gestures, smoothed across ten frames so the robot does not twitch.
  • Open palm forward, OK sign reverse, one finger left, two fingers right, fist stop.
  • Camera streaming on Windows and macOS through a Flask bridge into WSL2.
  • Publishes velocity commands on the ROS /cmd_vel topic.

Field hardware

IoT UGV for Remote Soil Health Sensing

An end-to-end precision agriculture rig: the vehicle gathers soil, environmental and GPS readings in the field and streams them back to a base station for analysis.

  • Raspberry Pi
  • Arduino Nano
  • NRF24
  • GPS
  • NPK / DHT11
  • Matplotlib
How it works
  • NPK, temperature and humidity sensors integrated alongside GPS tracking.
  • An NRF24 radio link carries readings from the vehicle back to base.
  • A Python pipeline processes, plots and stores the incoming CSV streams.
  • A local dashboard reports vehicle status, sensor values and position.

Open source

Automated CNC Potting Machine

An open-source precision farming rig on a CNC gantry that automates plowing, seeding and watering, designed to scale across crops and pot sizes.

  • Arduino Mega 2560
  • NEMA 17
  • A4988
  • Soil Moisture
  • IR Sensors
How it works
  • An Arduino Mega 2560 orchestrates the whole planting routine.
  • Moisture and IR sensors track soil condition and tray position in real time.
  • NEMA 17 steppers with A4988 drivers give precise tool positioning.
  • Open design, adaptable to different crops and pot sizes.
