Background

Education

I studied electronics and instrumentation before robotics was the plan. That order turned out to matter: sensors, signals and control loops were the vocabulary long before ROS was, and it means I tend to look at a misbehaving robot as an instrumentation problem first.

The master's at Buffalo filled in what that background could not — SLAM, modern perception, planning under uncertainty — and gave me a lab where simulation-first development was the house style. The certifications in between are mostly me shoring up the mathematics and the cloud side on my own time.

Useful accident

Five years between the bachelor's and the master's, spent shipping software and running a construction project. I would not trade them — they are why I care whether a robot can actually be maintained.
